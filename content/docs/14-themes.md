# Themes

Themes control how everything looks on the wall: typography, size, spacing,
alignment, colours, backgrounds and reference placement.

## The editor

The theme editor shows a **live preview** — you see the exact slide you are
styling while you change it, not an abstract swatch. Edit:

- **Typography** — typeface, size, spacing
- **Alignment** — how the text sits on the slide
- **Colours** — text and accent colours
- **Backgrounds** — solid colours or images behind the text
- **Reference placement** — where the citation (John 3:16) appears

## Themes freeze on air

This is the part that matters during a service: when a slide goes live, its
theme is **resolved and frozen into the slide**. Editing a theme afterwards
changes future slides — it can never restyle something already projected.

In practice: tweak the font mid-service and the verse that is on the wall
stays exactly as the congregation first saw it. The next verse picks up your
change. No flickering re-styles of on-air content, ever.

## Working with themes

- Theme choices apply to scripture slides, song sections and media
  backgrounds.
- The [slides setting](/docs/settings-reference#scripture-slides) decides
  whether a passage splits one-verse-per-slide or stays whole; the theme
  decides how each of those slides looks.
- Build your theme during rehearsal, not mid-service — though thanks to
  freezing, even a mid-service change is safe.

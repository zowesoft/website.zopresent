# Songs

Zopresent includes a song library built around how worship songs are
actually structured: **arrangements** made of named **sections** (verses,
choruses, bridges).

## The library

Store each song once with all its sections and lyric text. A song can carry
multiple **arrangements** — the order you actually sing it in — so the same
song serves a Sunday morning set and a midweek service without duplicating
text.

## Into the service

From the song library, add to the service order:

- a **whole arrangement** — every section, in order, as individual slides on
  the order, or
- a **single section** — just the second chorus you'll repeat, or the verse
  the preacher is about to reference.

Sections land as individual rows in the service order, so you step through
them with the same **←** / **→** keys as everything else, previewing each
one before it goes live.

## In a hurry

Because sections are first-class items, you can jump straight to the
chorus mid-song without hunting through the whole lyric sheet — select the
section, check the preview, send it live.

## Tips

- Keep section names short and recognisable ("Verse 2", "Chorus") — they
  become the labels you scan at speed during a service.
- If the band changes the order on the fly, build the arrangement as sung:
  duplicate sections in the arrangement where the song repeats them.
